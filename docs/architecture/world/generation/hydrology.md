# Hydrology

## Goal

Define static inland water and river decisions for generated natural terrain.

## Owns

- River path field selection.
- Riverbed carving hints.
- Static inland water column decisions.
- Drainage hints shared with coasts when water approaches sea level.

## Inputs

- Macro shape fields from [macro-fields.md](macro-fields.md).
- Absolute column coordinates.
- `world_seed`.
- Sea-level constants from [oceans-and-coasts.md](oceans-and-coasts.md) when
  ocean terrain is active.

## Outputs

- Riverbed depth and width hints.
- Static water fill decisions.
- Low-bank terrace hints around wet columns.
- Dryness hints consumed by [biome-pipeline.md](biome-pipeline.md).
- Surface constraints consumed by
  [surface-palettes.md](surface-palettes.md).

## Rules

1. Water generated by this stage is static terrain, not simulated fluid.
2. River continuity must hold across chunk borders without persisted neighbors.
3. River water uses the `72` top level owned by
   [../water-and-rivers.md](../water-and-rivers.md).
4. Riverbeds should be shallow lowland cuts, not water placed at the bottom of
   steep terrain cliffs.
5. Bank terraces must be part of terrain shaping before surface palettes run.
6. Spawn resolution must reject generated water columns.
7. Caves must not carve through static water columns.
8. Current static river behavior remains compatible with
   [../water-and-rivers.md](../water-and-rivers.md).
9. Hydrology may mark a column as wet, but final block IDs are selected by the
   surface or ocean owner.

## Verification

- Golden tests include fixed-seed river and dry-column samples.
- Golden tests include a bank sample whose dry surface is near the water top.
- Border property tests cover river continuity across adjacent chunks.
- Live chunk smoke checks decoded water blocks when river terrain is active.

## Out Of Scope

- Fluid spread, flow ticks, buckets, boats, swimming, and aquatic mobs.
- Full erosion simulation.
- Underground aquifers.
